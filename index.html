(02) ABOUT

Who am I?

Hey, I’m Feena Chisholm (O’Sullivan), an AI-first product designer who builds and crafts user-centered product experiences. I have 10+ years in B2B SaaS, with companies like HubSpot, Miro, Zendesk and Workday. I am currently based in Dublin, Ireland.

I have startup experience too. My husband Cormac is building DevAlly, an AI accessibility (a11y) startup, and it’s been quite a journey.

I love working with smart, empowered people, and care about building experiences that are both impactful and delightful. My background spans graphic design, design systems, information architecture and systems thinking. Always happy to grab a coffee (or a spicy marg) and talk product.
